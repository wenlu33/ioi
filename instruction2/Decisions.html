Part 2

All decisions will be made using the computer.
Please read the instructions below carefully, as they explain the task and how your earnings will be determined.

On Your Screen

You are presented with Box A and Box B.
The computer places one ball in each box.
The ball in Box A has an unknown color, either YELLOW or BLUE.

New in Part 2

Before making your choice, you will receive a Recommendation Ball that recommends Box A or Box B.

See details below on how recommendations are generated.

Each round, there are 5 players, and the computer prepares 5 private Recommendation Balls — one per person.

How the Recommendation Ball is Generated

If the Ball in Box A is YELLOW:

  • All 5 of you receive Recommendation Balls labeled "A"

If the Ball in Box A is BLUE:

  • 3 of you receive Recommendation Balls labeled "A"
  • 2 of you receive Recommendation Balls labeled "B"

Each participant is randomly given one of these Recommendation Balls and sees only their own.

Decision Task

Each round in Part 2 will proceed in the following order:

1. You Receive Your Recommendation Ball

The computer will draw a Recommendation Ball for you.
You will see only your own Recommendation Ball (either A or B).

2. You will choose either Box A or Box B

Earnings

If you choose Box A:

  • YELLOW → You earn 30 ECU
  • BLUE → You earn 10 ECU

If you choose Box B:

  • The earnings depend on how many others also chose Box B.
