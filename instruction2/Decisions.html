On Your Screen

- You are presented with Box A and Box B.
- The computer places one ball in each box.
- The ball in Box A has an unknown color, either YELLOW or BLUE.
- The ball in Box B depends on how many participants choose Box B.
- No Clue Balls are provided in this part.

Belief and Decision Task
  1. Guess Task:

    You will be asked:
    “What is your best guess about the probability that the Ball in Box A is YELLOW?”

    You will enter a number between 0 and 100, where:

    • 0 = completely sure it is BLUE
    • 100 = completely sure it is YELLOW
    • 50 = equally likely to be YELLOW or BLUE

  2. Decision Task:

    After entering your guess, you will choose either Box A or Box B.

Earnings

If you choose Box A:

  • YELLOW → You earn 3 ECU
  • BLUE → You earn 1 ECU
If you choose Box B:
  • The earnings depend on how many others also chose Box B.
